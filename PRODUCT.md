# TCF Studios — Production Management System

## Product Purpose
A production management system for TCF Studios, a content studio specialising in short-form vertical video for brand clients. Covers the end-to-end workflow from brand brief and creator matching through scripting, production, and episode delivery across relevant social media channels.

## Register
product

## Users

### Admin (TJ — founder/director)
- Uses on desktop and on-set on mobile phone
- Needs to see everything at once: all brands, creators, seasons, episodes
- Hates admin overhead — wants maximum automation, minimum clicks
- Creative director mindset: visual, cinematic, high standards

### Brand clients
- Marketing managers and brand leads at consumer companies
- Used to working with premium agencies
- Need to feel confident their investment is being managed professionally
- Want clear campaign status, easy approvals, clean invoices

### Creators (talent)
- Social media creators with 100K–1M+ audiences
- Native to TikTok/Instagram aesthetic
- Need to feel like they're working with a real studio, not a spreadsheet
- Approve scripts, track shoot dates, see their live stats

## Brand & Tone

**TCF Studios is a proper studio — not a startup, not an agency, not a freelancer.**

- Dark, cinematic, confident. The aesthetic of a real production company.
- Professional but not corporate. Creative but not chaotic.
- Typography is bold and tight. White space is intentional.
- Colour: deep navy/black backgrounds (#0A0A0A, #0D0D1A), violet-to-blue gradient accents for primary actions
- Status colours: emerald for approved/active, amber for pending/in-progress, red for overdue, zinc for draft/inactive

## Anti-references
- No startup-purple SaaS look (Notion, Linear clones)
- No corporate blue enterprise feel
- No over-decorated gradients everywhere — gradients are accents, not wallpaper
- No rounded bubbly components
- No emoji in UI

## Strategic Principles
- Information density over whitespace padding — TJ needs to see a lot at once
- Dark mode first for admin; light mode for brand portal
- Mobile responsive is non-negotiable — used on set
- Status is always visible — every item shows where it is in the pipeline at a glance
- Automation reduces TJ's admin burden — the UI should reflect that things happen automatically
